The Three Buttons Apple Never Had the Guts to Remove

Watch the public investigation ↗
Public question
Why did the three coloured window controls survive every macOS redesign while equally bold Windows interfaces disappeared?
Evidence
The origins of Aqua, criticism of early Mac OS X, the competing histories of Windows Luna and Metro, twenty-five years of design continuity, and the controls' later move to iPadOS.
Verdict
A detail becomes durable not simply because it is usable or attractive, but because a company repeatedly protects it as a marker of product identity.
Product implication
When replacing an established interface, distinguish obsolete mechanics from the details that carry continuity and recognition. Removing both is not simplification.

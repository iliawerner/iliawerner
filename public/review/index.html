Written intake preview

The decision should be understandable without a meeting.

  1. State the interface decision in one sentence.
  2. Name the two or three live alternatives.
  3. Define the user group, operating context, decision date and practical constraint.
  4. Attach the evidence and internal arguments already available.

I return a short written framing of the decision, evidence sufficiency, evaluation criteria and planned research path. You approve it before the ten-day stress-test begins or receive a full refund.
